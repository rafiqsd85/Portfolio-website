My commitments toward a more active, collaborative, and engaging PCIU Computer Club.

My Vision

A club where everyone gets a chance to grow.

I believe a successful computer club is built on teamwork, creativity, active participation, and a shared passion for technology. I aim to contribute to an inclusive environment where students can learn, share ideas, and grow together.

01

Responsibility

Active Participation

I will participate actively in club meetings, events, and activities while completing assigned tasks sincerely and respecting deadlines.

Show up. Contribute. Take responsibility.
02

Growth & Innovation

Technical Events

I will support coding workshops, programming contests, tech talks, quizzes, and project showcases that encourage technical learning and creative thinking.

Learn new skills. Explore new ideas.
03

Collaboration

Teamwork & Engagement

Every member matters.

Connect. Compete. Grow together.
